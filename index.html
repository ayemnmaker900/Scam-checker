      Been scammed? Do this
      1. Stop all contact with the scammer. Do not send more money or information.
      2. Call your bank or mobile wallet provider right away to block your card, account, or SIM and try to stop the payment.
      3. Change the passwords of your email, banking, and social media accounts, and turn on two-factor authentication.
      4. Save evidence: screenshots of messages, links, phone numbers, and payment receipts. Do not delete anything.
      5. Report it in Pakistan: call the cyber crime helpline 1991 (FIA / NCCIA) or file a complaint online, and report the number or account on the platform it came from. For harassment or blackmail, the Digital Rights Foundation helpline is 0800-39393. In an emergency, call the police on 15.
        6. Tell a trusted family member or friend. Scams are not your fault, and talking about it helps you recover and protects others.

      This tool uses simple rules, so it can miss scams or flag safe messages. Never share OTPs, PINs, or passwords, and confirm with your bank or the company through its official number.
